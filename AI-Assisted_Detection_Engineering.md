# AI-Assisted Detection Engineering

## Use AI For

### Detection Rule Suggestions
Artificial intelligence can analyze large volumes of telemetry and threat intelligence to propose new detection rules. By identifying recurring patterns and anomalies, AI helps engineers design rules that target emerging threats more quickly than manual methods.

### ATT&CK Mapping Assistance
AI can automatically map detection rules and alerts to MITRE ATT&CK techniques. This reduces manual effort and ensures that detections are consistently aligned with adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures. Automated mapping also highlights gaps in coverage across the ATT&CK matrix.

### Detection Coverage Reviews
AI can evaluate existing detection rules against organizational requirements and frameworks. By comparing rules to known attack techniques, AI highlights areas of strong coverage and identifies where detections are missing. This accelerates coverage assessments and supports continuous improvement.

### Use Case Generation
AI can generate new detection use cases by analyzing threat intelligence feeds, attack simulations, and historical incidents. These use cases provide defenders with practical scenarios to test and validate detection logic, ensuring that rules remain relevant to real-world threats.

### Alert Tuning Recommendations
AI can review alert data to recommend adjustments that reduce noise and false positives. By learning from analyst feedback and historical alert outcomes, AI suggests threshold changes, contextual filters, or risk scoring improvements. This helps SOC teams focus on meaningful alerts.

---

## Validate Outputs

### Verify Detection Logic
Even when AI generates detection rules, engineers must verify that the logic is technically correct and aligned with organizational goals. Verification ensures that rules trigger under the right conditions and do not introduce operational risks.

### Review Operational Impact
AI-assisted detections must be reviewed for their impact on SOC workflows. Analysts should confirm that alerts are actionable, properly prioritized, and integrated into existing processes. Operational review prevents AI-generated rules from overwhelming teams with impractical alerts.

### Assess False Positive Risks
AI outputs must be evaluated for false positive risks. While AI can reduce noise, it may also generate overly broad rules that trigger on benign activity. Analysts should test rules against real data to confirm that false positives are minimized and that detections remain trustworthy.

---

# Key Takeaways
- AI enhances detection engineering by suggesting rules, mapping to ATT&CK, reviewing coverage, generating use cases, and tuning alerts.  
- Human validation is essential to verify detection logic, review operational impact, and assess false positive risks.  
- AI-assisted detection engineering accelerates rule development while maintaining accuracy and operational relevance.
